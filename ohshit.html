GOOM 꿈

David Choi is a new research engineer at Loosid, an AI tech company that allows you to review and analyze your dreams in order to train yourself to lucid dream. Work all day, and live your dreams at night. As David tests this technology on himself he struggles to discern what is reality and what is dream. The blurring of the line between dream and reality forces David to confront uncomfortable truths about his aging Korean mother, racism in the workplace, illusions of the American dream, and his deep dissatisfaction with his own life.
